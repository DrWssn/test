---
name: Medical image privacy
description: Privacy constraint for photo attachments in the medical-notes app.
---

Device and clipboard images attached to medical notes must remain private in the user's Google Drive. The Google Doc should link to the private Drive file; do not grant public access to the image.

**Why:** Medical images can contain sensitive health information, so making uploads publicly accessible would be an unacceptable privacy change.

**How to apply:** Preserve private Drive permissions in any future attachment, sharing, or image-preview work, and verify the document link does not change the image's access level.
