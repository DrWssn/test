MEDICAL NOTES

Capture clearly.

Medical Notes is an Android app for filing clinical notes into your own Google Doc. Write a note, add a specialty tag such as #cardiology, and the app saves it to that specialty's tab in your document, each note on its own page.

How it works

  • You sign in with your Google account on your phone.
  • You choose an existing Google Doc or create a new one.
  • Notes go straight from your phone to Google Docs. There is no Medical Notes server.
  • Images you attach are uploaded privately to your own Google Drive and linked from the note.
