MEDICAL NOTES

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Medical Notes ("the app") is an Android app that saves your notes into a Google Doc you choose. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

No server

The app has no backend server. Everything it does happens on your phone, which talks directly to Google's APIs. The developer never receives your notes, documents, images, or Google account data.

Google account data the app uses

When you sign in, you grant the app access to your Google Drive and Google Docs. The app uses that access only to:

  • list your Google Docs so you can pick where notes are saved;
  • read the chosen document's title and tabs;
  • create new documents and tabs, and add your notes to them;
  • upload images you attach to your own Drive, without sharing them, and link them from the note.

The app also reads your Google account email address to show which account is signed in.

Data stored on your phone

The app stores the ID of your chosen Google Doc on your phone so it remembers it between launches. Google sign-in tokens are managed by Google Play services on your phone and are sent only to Google.

Sharing and selling

The app does not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking. It does not use Google user data to train AI or machine-learning models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes the document ID stored on your phone. Notes and images already saved stay in your own Google Drive, where you control them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/DrWssn/test/issues.
